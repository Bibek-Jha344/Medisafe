Everything You Need for Better Health

MEDISAFE provides a comprehensive suite of healthcare tools designed for patients seeking quality care and physiotherapy support.

🔍

Symptom Assessment

Describe your symptoms through our structured assessment. Our rule-based system evaluates your input and provides intelligent recommendations.

👨‍⚕️

Doctor Recommendations

Based on your symptoms and body area affected, MEDISAFE recommends the most suitable medical specialists in your area.

📅

Appointment Booking

Book appointments with qualified doctors in just a few clicks. View availability, choose time slots, and manage all your appointments easily.

🏃

Physiotherapy Guidance

Access guided physiotherapy exercises with step-by-step instructions, safety guidance, and progress tracking for effective recovery.

💊

Prescription Management

Securely store, view, and manage your prescriptions. Track medications, dosages, and follow-up schedules all in one place.

📊

Progress Tracking

Monitor your recovery journey with detailed progress charts, joint angle analysis, exercise completion tracking, and recovery scores.

How MEDISAFE Works

A simple, guided process from symptom assessment to recovery support.

1
Register & Login
Create your patient account with basic health information to get personalized healthcare support.
2
Describe Symptoms
Use our structured symptom assessment to describe your condition including body area, pain level, and duration.
3
Get Recommendations
Our rule-based engine analyzes your inputs and recommends the appropriate specialist and physiotherapy support.
4
Book Appointment
Schedule a consultation with the recommended doctor at a time that suits you.
5
Follow Treatment
Access guided exercises, manage prescriptions, and track your recovery progress over time.
